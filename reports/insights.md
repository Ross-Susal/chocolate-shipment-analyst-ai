# Project 1 — Chocolate Shipment Analysis

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes 25,073 chocolate shipments to understand revenue,
profitability, product performance, regional performance, sales performance,
cancellations, and time-based trends.

## Key Findings

### 1. Overall Performance

- Total Revenue: 141.49 million
- Total Shipments: 25,073
- Cancelled Shipments: 1,215
- Cancellation Rate: 4.85%

### 2. Regional Performance

- APAC generated the highest revenue and profit.
- APAC generated approximately 100.33 million in revenue.
- Americas generated approximately 30.93 million in revenue.
- Europe generated approximately 10.23 million in revenue.
- Regional profit margins were relatively similar, with APAC slightly ahead
  of Americas and Europe.

### 3. Product Performance

- Organic Choco Syrup generated the highest absolute profit.
- Caramel Stuffed Bars was another major profit contributor.
- Peanut Butter Cubes had the highest profit margin at approximately 90.26%.
- Raspberry Choco also had a very high profit margin of approximately 84.70%.
- 85% Dark Bars was the largest loss-making product.
- Baker's Choco Chips and After Nines also generated negative profit.

### 4. Loss-Making Products

- 85% Dark Bars generated a significant loss across all regions.
- 85% Dark Bars remained loss-making in 2023, 2024, and 2025.
- Baker's Choco Chips and After Nines also generated losses.
- These products should be investigated for pricing, cost structure,
  or operational issues.

### 5. Cancellation Analysis

- Overall cancellation rate was 4.85%.
- APAC had the highest cancellation rate among the regions at approximately
  4.89%.
- Caramel Stuffed Bars had the highest product-level cancellation rate at
  approximately 5.74%.
- Peanut Butter Cubes had the lowest product-level cancellation rate at
  approximately 3.83%.

### 6. Salesperson Performance

- Ponnan Delhi generated the highest absolute profit.
- Ponnan Delhi ranked #1 by profit.
- Gopal Venkata had the highest profit margin at approximately 59.80%.
- Ramalingam Kothapeta ranked #2 by profit margin.
- Profit rank and margin rank were not the same, showing that high sales
  volume and high efficiency are different performance dimensions.

### 7. Team Performance

- Delish generated the highest revenue and absolute profit.
- Yummies had the highest profit margin among the teams.
- Team profit margins were relatively close to one another.

### 8. Time-Based Performance

- 2023 Revenue: approximately 63.85 million.
- 2024 Revenue: approximately 62.30 million.
- 2025 Revenue: approximately 15.35 million.

- 2024 had a slightly higher profit margin than 2023.
- Revenue showed stronger performance during several year-end months.
- 2025 should not be directly compared with 2023 and 2024 as a full-year
  performance period because the dataset only contains January–March 2025.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the cost structure and pricing of 85% Dark Bars.
2. Investigate why Baker's Choco Chips and After Nines are loss-making.
3. Study high-margin products such as Peanut Butter Cubes and Raspberry Choco
   to understand what drives their profitability.
4. Investigate the relatively high cancellation rate in APAC.
5. Analyze seasonal revenue patterns to improve inventory and sales planning.
6. Compare salesperson performance using both absolute profit and profit margin
   rather than relying on a single ranking.
